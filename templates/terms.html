🇺🇸 U.S. Student Visa Assistance Service

The U.S. Student Visa Application Assistance service is a separate, optional, paid service. Africa ScholarBridge charges a service fee (shown at checkout — US$11.59 / KSh 1,500 for Kenya). Under this service model, after that payment is verified, Africa ScholarBridge separately arranges and covers the applicable U.S. government visa application processing fee (currently US$185) for eligible students — you do not pay Africa ScholarBridge a second amount for this.

U.S. Student Visa Fee Coverage

Students who purchase the Africa ScholarBridge U.S. Student Visa Application Assistance service for US$11.59 / KSh 1,500 are eligible for Africa ScholarBridge to cover the applicable US$185 U.S. student visa application processing fee under the terms of this service.

The US$185 fee is a government visa application processing fee and is separate from the Africa ScholarBridge service fee.

Africa ScholarBridge's payment of the applicable visa application processing fee does not guarantee visa approval or issuance.

Visa eligibility and final visa decisions remain with the appropriate U.S. government authorities.

Processing Timeline

The "up to 2 weeks" timeline refers only to Africa ScholarBridge's own assistance process once payment is verified and required information is received. It is not a promise of U.S. government visa approval or of any specific U.S. government appointment or processing timeline.

Refund Policy

Whether the Africa ScholarBridge service fee (US$11.59 / KSh 1,500) is refundable is determined case by case, considering: payment status, application status, whether assistance work has already started, and whether the US$185 government fee has already been paid on your behalf. Africa ScholarBridge does not automatically refund the US$185 government fee once paid, unless the applicable official U.S. government payment process allows a refund - that fee then follows the U.S. government's own refund rules, not ours.

External Provider & Government Decisions

Africa ScholarBridge assistance — for both funding matching and visa applications — is separate from decisions made by external scholarship providers, universities, governments, or U.S. immigration authorities. We do not control or guarantee those outcomes.
