About Africa ScholarBridge

Africa ScholarBridge helps African students discover, prepare for, and connect with legitimate education funding opportunities through one centralized annual funding application.

We are more than a scholarship directory. Africa ScholarBridge is a centralized Annual Application + Funding Matching + Application Tracking + Education Funding Information Platform.

Our Mission

To bridge the gap between African students and the education funding opportunities they deserve - through one simple annual application, transparent matching, and clear tracking every step of the way.

How We Work

Submit once each funding cycle. Get reviewed. Get matched. Get connected to legitimate funding providers. We are not a guaranteed-scholarship service - we help you find and prepare for real opportunities from real organizations.

🇺🇸 U.S. Student Visa Assistance

For students heading to the United States, Africa ScholarBridge also offers a separate, optional U.S. Student Visa Application Assistance service - practical guidance through your DS-160, documents, and interview preparation, connected to the same student account as your funding application.

What We Are Not

Africa ScholarBridge is not a scholarship provider, a university, a government agency, or an official U.S. visa portal, and we do not make funding or visa decisions - those belong to the relevant funding providers, institutions, and immigration authorities. We do not process the U.S. government's own visa fee; that is paid directly to the U.S. government through official channels.
