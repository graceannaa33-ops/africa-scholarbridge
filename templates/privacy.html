Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

We collect account information (name, email, phone), education details, and information you provide in your annual funding application, including your personal statement and financial-need details.

Why We Collect It

Your information is used to match you with relevant funding opportunities, track your application, and communicate updates about your funding journey.

Account Security

Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing and are never stored in plain text. Please use a strong, unique password for your account.

Application Information

Your annual application is stored securely and is only accessible to you and authorized Africa ScholarBridge administrators reviewing your file.

Document Handling

Uploaded documents are handled securely and used solely for application review and provider referral purposes.

Cookies

We use session cookies to keep you logged in. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

Data Retention

Application data is retained across annual cycles so you can view your history and pre-fill future applications, unless you request deletion.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us.

🇺🇸 Visa Assistance Information

If you use the U.S. Student Visa Application Assistance service, we additionally collect visa-relevant information you provide (passport status, visa category, education and financial-source information, and documents such as your passport, DS-160 confirmation, and I-20). This information is used solely to provide the assistance service you requested and is accessible only to you and authorized administrators reviewing your visa request.

Payment Information

Payment records (amount, currency, payment method, and transaction reference) are stored to verify and reconcile payments for the visa assistance service fee. Africa ScholarBridge does not store full card numbers; any external payment processor's own privacy practices apply to information you provide directly to them.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use our Contact page.
